📘 Mathematics

📐 Geometry

Geometry studies shapes, angles, lines and figures. Area of a rectangle = length × breadth. Area of a square = side × side. Perimeter of a rectangle = 2 × (length + breadth). Perimeter of a square = 4 × side.

📊 Algebra

Algebra uses symbols and equations to solve mathematical problems. Variables represent unknown or changing values. An equation states that two expressions are equal.

📏 Trigonometry

Trigonometry studies relationships between angles and sides of triangles using sine, cosine and tangent.

🧮 Arithmetic Expression

An arithmetic expression combines numbers and operations such as addition, subtraction, multiplication and division.

📊 Ratio and Proportion

A ratio compares two quantities. A proportion states that two ratios are equal.

⚛️ Physics

🌎 Gravity

Gravity is the force that attracts objects towards Earth and keeps planets in orbit. The theory of gravity was first introduced by Sir Isaac Newton.

⚡ Electricity

Electricity is the flow of electric charge used to power devices and machines. Electricity has 2 charges negative and positive.

🔊 Sound

Sound is a form of energy produced by vibrations travelling through air. Sound is measured using Hertz (Hz) this determines how high or low the sound is. The of a sound wave is measured in decibles (dB). Prolonged exposure to sounds more than 85 (dB) can cause hearing damage.

🔬 Chemistry

🧪 Periodic Table

The periodic table arranges chemical elements based on atomic number and properties. For Example, Sodium-Na Lithium-Li Calcium-Ca Copper-Cu Silver-Ag Gold-Au Hydrogen-[H]

⚗️ Acids Bases and Salts

Acids are sour, bases are bitter and salts are formed through chemical reactions. When acids and bases are mixed they form salts. This process is called neutralization. There are different types of acids and bases too! Organic acids are the acids which are present in plants and animals while mineral acids are artificially produced in industries. Organic acids are weak whereas mineral acids are weak. Some common organic acids are Citric acid, Malic acid and Lactic acid. Some common mineral acids are Sulfuric acid and Nitric acid. The bases which are soluble in water are called alkalis. Indicators are the substances that change colour in the presence of acid or a base.

🌍 Geography

🏔️ Mountains

Mountains are large elevated landforms formed through tectonic plate movements. Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world. Forests which are found in mountains are called Montane Forest. Mounatains roughly cover about 27% of the Earth's land area. They regulate global wheather and are primary source of freshwater for billions of people. FUN FACT: While Mount Everest is the highest mountain above sea level, Mount Kea is technically the tallest mountain if measured from its true base on the ocean floor.

🌊 Oceans

Oceans cover most of Earth’s surface and help regulate climate and weather. There are 5 oceans in the world they are Pacific ocean, Atlantic ocean, Indian ocean, Arctic ocean, Southern ocean. Pacific ocean is the largest ocean in the world. FUN FACT: Indian ocean is the only ocean named after a country.

🌧️Weather and Climate

Climate describes long-term weather patterns of a region over many years. Weather is the short-term state of the atmosphere.
